Our Mission

We are Group 17, a team of computer science students learning by building together. Our goal is to create useful applications while improving our programming, problem-solving, and communication skills. We value respect, responsibility, curiosity, and helping each other succeed.

Our Values

We welcome different ideas, learn from mistakes, and take responsibility for our contributions. Every team member deserves to be heard and supported.

Proposed Team Rules

  • Listen respectfully and let everyone contribute.
  • Divide tasks fairly and agree on deadlines.
  • Share progress and report delays early.
  • Ask for help when stuck and support teammates.
  • Review code together before merging changes.
  • Each member makes at least one meaningful commit.
  • Give credit to everyone who contributes.
